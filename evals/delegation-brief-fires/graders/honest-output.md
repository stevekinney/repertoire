The brief specifies the expected output shape: ordered fields, evidence per claim (file and line, command and exit code), observed facts kept apart from inferences, and a status of complete, partial, or blocked. It permits the honest outcomes of supported findings, no supported findings, or not enough evidence, and sets no quota or minimum number of findings. It also says who weighs and verifies the result rather than trusting the worker's own confidence.
